      MANA TABLE · 0.1.0 BETA 28

      Build with the full bundled card library, practice opening hands, and play Constructed or Commander tables with up to five AI opponents.

      The engine handles turns, spells, targets, mana, and combat. Commander uses 40 life, the command zone, commander tax, and commander damage. Unusual card interactions need broader testing. Games end when you close the app.

      Format checks cover deck structure. Rotating set legality and ban lists are not checked. Your decks save locally after each edit.

      Card illustrations load from Scryfall when available and are cached for offline use. Illustrations may show a different printing from the deck entry.
